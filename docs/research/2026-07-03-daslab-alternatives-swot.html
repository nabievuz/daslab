Founder-level yakuniy natija

Hech bir OSS loyiha DasLab'ning to'rt ustunini birlashtirmaydi — gated governance (begona maksimum: BMAD 7), spec→build kompilyator (MetaGPT/BMAD 8 — lekin dvigatelsiz/muzlagan), typed kontraktlar va per-agent evals hech qayerda birga kelmaydi. DasLab 1.0 (59/120) hozirning o'zida 12 ishtirokchidan #3; ORGANISM bajarilsa (115/120) eng yaqin raqibdan 45+ ball uzoqlashadi. Sektor patterni — org-qatlam qiymati yopiladi (MetaGPT→Atoms, GPT-Engineer→Lovable $12B muzokara, CAMEL→Eigent, Letta→Constellation): tezis tasdiqlandi, lekin vaqt hisoblangan.

Eng qimmatli importlar: Letta MemFS (git-backed xotira → ArcRift), OpenHands regression-eval intizomi, AgentScope interrupt-service + OTel, BMAD step-file + scale-adaptive routing, CAMEL FailureHandlingConfig, Agent Zero snapshot/rollback. Ogohlantirishlar: Ruflo yulduziga aldanmaslik (fabrikatsiya auditlari), AutoGPT Polyform (OSS emas), OWL LICENSE'siz.
